Privacy Policy

Bible Streak  •  Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Bible Streak ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

You can use the App in one of three ways, each with different information collected:

  • Guest (Anonymous): No email or personal details are required. The App creates an anonymous account identifier and asks you to choose a display name (nickname) shown on the in-app leaderboard.
  • Google Sign-In: If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your basic Google account name and email address, provided to us by Google, to create your account and set your default display name.
  • Email Sign-Up: If you create an account with an email address and password, we collect your email address, a hashed password (via Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your password in plain text), and the display name you choose.

1.2 Content You Create

The App lets you create bookmarks, notes, and highlights on Bible verses, and track progress on reading plans. This content is stored on your device. If you are a Premium user, this content is also securely synced to our cloud database (Firebase Cloud Firestore) so it can be restored if you switch devices or reinstall the App.

1.3 Quiz Activity & Leaderboard

Your quiz scores are recorded and combined into a monthly points total, associated with your chosen display name. Your display name and points are visible to other users of the App on the public in-app leaderboard. Please choose a display name that does not reveal your real identity if you'd prefer to remain private.

1.4 Purchase Information

If you choose to support the App's development through the "Support the Developer" feature, your purchase is processed entirely by Google Play's billing system. We never receive or store your card or payment details — we only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, so we can acknowledge your support.

1.5 Technical & Usage Information

Like most apps built on Firebase, the App may automatically collect standard technical information such as app version, general usage patterns, and crash/error reports, used only to keep the App stable and to understand which features are useful.

1.6 Advertising Information

The free version of the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob (banner ads and optional rewarded video ads). To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Premium users do not see ads and no ad requests are made for them.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate core features: Bible reading, bookmarks, notes, highlights, reading plans, quizzes, and the leaderboard.
  • To let you sign in and keep your data connected to your account.
  • To sync your content across devices (Premium users only).
  • To process and acknowledge voluntary support/donations via Google Play.
  • To send account-related emails (such as email verification or password reset), if you use email sign-up.
  • To show ads to non-Premium users and to let you unlock optional rewards (such as bonus quiz questions) by watching rewarded ads.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the App.

3. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services to operate. Each processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — manages sign-in for Guest, Google, and Email accounts.
  • Cloud Firestore (Google Firebase) — stores leaderboard entries and, for Premium users, cloud-synced bookmarks/notes/highlights.
  • Google Sign-In (Google) — used only if you choose to sign in with your Google account.
  • Google Play Billing (Google) — processes all in-app support/donation payments.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google) — serves ads to non-Premium users and may use your advertising ID. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) (Google) — shows a consent message in the European Economic Area and the UK and records your ad choices.

You can review Google's own privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase's cloud infrastructure, which applies industry-standard security measures. Reading, notes, bookmarks, and highlights for Guest and non-Premium users stay only on your device unless you upgrade to Premium and cloud sync is enabled. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Ad data (such as your advertising ID) is processed by Google AdMob only to serve and measure ads, as described in Section 6. The only information visible to other users of the App is your chosen display name and your quiz leaderboard points. We may disclose information if required to do so by law.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • Sign out at any time from Settings → Profile & Leaderboard.
  • Delete your account at any time from Settings → Profile & Leaderboard → Delete Account. This permanently removes your leaderboard entry, cloud-synced data, and authentication record, and cannot be undone.
  • Stay fully local by using the App as a Guest without upgrading to Premium — your personal content (notes, bookmarks, highlights) will never leave your device.
  • Ad choices: In the EEA and UK you can review or change your ad consent at any time from Settings → Privacy Options. On Android you can also reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Google → Ads. Upgrading to Premium removes ads entirely.
  • You may also contact us directly (see below) to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the App evolves. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.
